Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my subscription on a new device?

Open the app, go to Settings, and tap "Restore Purchases". Your subscription is linked to your Apple ID and will be restored automatically.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. Go to iPhone/iPad Settings → your name → Subscriptions, then select EPSO Prep to cancel or modify.

The app is not loading or crashes on startup

Try closing and reopening the app. If the issue persists, make sure you have the latest version installed from the App Store, then restart your device.

How does the adaptive learning engine work?

EPSO Prep uses Item Response Theory (IRT) to estimate your ability level and select questions that match your current proficiency. The more you practice, the more accurate your readiness score becomes.

Can I change my language or exam tier after onboarding?

Yes. Go to Settings inside the app to change your preferred language, subscription tier, or specialty field at any time.

I found an incorrect question or answer

We take content accuracy seriously. Please email us at support.epsoprep@eu-now.com with the question text or ID and we will review it promptly.

Bug Reports

When reporting a bug, please include:

  • Your device model and iOS/iPadOS version
  • The app version (found in Settings → About)
  • Steps to reproduce the issue
  • A screenshot if possible

System Requirements

  • iOS 17.0 or later / iPadOS 17.0 or later
  • iPhone or iPad
  • Internet connection required for Sign in with Apple and subscription management
