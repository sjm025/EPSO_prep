1.About this Policy

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") explains how EPSO Prep (the "App") processes personal data of its users ("you", "User"). It applies to the iOS application, the associated websites and any related services (the "Service").

By using the Service you acknowledge this Policy. If you do not agree, do not use the Service. This Policy should be read together with our Terms of Service.

2.Data Controller

The data controller for the processing described here is:

  • Jesús Rodríguez Albarca, sole trader established in Spain, trading as EPSO Prep.
  • Contact: privacy@euexamprep.app

For requests concerning the exercise of your rights, see section 14.

3.Data we process

3.1 Account data

  • Apple user identifier: a stable, pseudonymous string provided by Apple when you sign in with Sign in with Apple. It is not an email address. We store it securely in the device Keychain.
  • Display name (optional): provided only if you choose to share it at sign-in.

3.2 Purchase data

  • Subscription entitlement: which tier (Free, CAST, AST, AD5, AD7) you currently hold. Derived locally from Apple's StoreKit transaction history.
  • Product and transaction identifiers: handled by Apple; we do not receive your payment details, card numbers or billing address.

3.3 Product interaction data

  • Practice activity: which questions you answered, whether your answer was correct, response time in milliseconds, ability estimate (theta), and streak counters. Stored exclusively on your device in Documents/eu-exam-prep/progress.json.
  • Preferences: language, appearance (system/light/dark), specialty, notification preferences. Stored in UserDefaults on the device.

3.4 Technical data processed in transit

  • Requests to our question back-end carry standard HTTP/TLS metadata (IP, user agent, timestamp) for the sole purpose of delivering the response. We do not associate this data with your identity or store it beyond what is necessary to operate the service, and logs are kept only for diagnostic purposes for up to 30 days by our hosting provider.

3.5 Notifications

  • If you enable daily practice reminders, we schedule local notifications on your device. No token is ever sent to our servers.

4.What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your email address, phone number, physical address or date of birth.
  • We do not collect your precise or approximate location.
  • We do not collect device advertising identifiers (IDFA). We do not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission because we do not track.
  • We do not collect biometric, health, financial or special-category data under Art. 9 GDPR.
  • We do not collect contacts, photos, camera, microphone or device files.
  • We do not embed third-party analytics SDKs, A/B-testing SDKs or advertising SDKs.

5.Purposes of processing

  • Authenticating you via Sign in with Apple and maintaining your session.
  • Providing the adaptive practice experience (question selection driven by IRT).
  • Persisting your progress so it survives app restarts.
  • Delivering question content from the back-end and caching it for offline use.
  • Managing your subscription entitlement so paid features are available.
  • Sending local reminder notifications you explicitly opted in to.
  • Detecting and preventing fraud, abuse and security incidents.
  • Complying with our legal obligations.

7.Children under 16

The Service is directed at adults and young adults preparing for EU institution selection procedures. It is not directed at children under 16. If you become aware that a minor has used the Service without the authorisation of a parent or guardian, please contact us and we will delete the account.

8.Retention

  • On your device: data is kept as long as the App is installed. Uninstalling removes all local progress. You can clear data earlier by using the "Reset progress" option in the App or by deleting and reinstalling.
  • On our servers: the Apple user identifier is never stored server-side. Subscription state is derived on device from Apple. Hosting-provider logs are retained for up to 30 days for operational and security purposes.
  • After you stop using the Service: there is nothing we hold that is linked to you, because the primary identifier stays on your device in the Keychain.

10.International transfers

Processing is primarily located in the European Union. Where any processor transfers data outside the EU/EEA (for example Apple in the United States), such transfers are protected by the appropriate safeguards under Chapter V GDPR, including Standard Contractual Clauses and, where applicable, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

11.Automated decision-making

The App uses a 3-Parameter Logistic IRT model to select the next question adapted to your estimated ability. This is an educational adaptation, not a decision that produces legal effects or significantly affects you within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR. We do not profile you for any purpose beyond adapting practice difficulty on your device.

12.Security measures

  • The Apple user identifier is stored in the iOS Keychain with kSecAttrAccessibleAfterFirstUnlock.
  • All network traffic uses HTTPS/TLS.
  • Minimal-data approach: we do not hold server-side user profiles.
  • Internal access is limited to the sole trader acting as controller.
  • Regular review of dependencies and hosting provider security posture.

No system is 100% secure. If you become aware of a vulnerability, please report it at security@euexamprep.app.

13.Your rights (Arts. 12–22 GDPR)

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data (Art. 15).
  • Rectify inaccurate data (Art. 16).
  • Erase your data ("right to be forgotten", Art. 17). Note that uninstalling the App already removes local data; for any residual data contact us at section 14.
  • Restrict processing (Art. 18).
  • Data portability (Art. 20): receive your progress data as a machine-readable JSON export.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21).
  • Not be subject to automated decision-making with legal effects (Art. 22) — not applicable here, see section 11.
  • Withdraw consent at any time (for processing based on consent); withdrawal does not affect lawfulness of prior processing.

14.How to exercise your rights

Send an email to privacy@euexamprep.app. We will respond within one month as required by Art. 12 GDPR. To help us identify you without requesting excessive data, please include the Apple user identifier displayed in the "About" section of the App (or, alternatively, a screenshot of the App's Help screen).

Exercising your rights is free of charge. In manifestly unfounded or excessive cases we may charge a reasonable fee or refuse to act, as permitted by Art. 12(5).

15.Right to lodge a complaint

If you consider that our processing infringes the GDPR, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority of your Member State of residence. In Spain:

  • Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD)
  • C/ Jorge Juan, 6, 28001 Madrid

  • The website (these legal pages) is a static site with no tracking cookies, no analytics scripts and no fingerprinting.
  • The App does not embed third-party SDKs for analytics, attribution or advertising.
